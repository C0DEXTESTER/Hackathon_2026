About ResearchGuard AI

A privacy-first similarity checker for research papers — built for educators, reviewers and students.

🔒

100% Private

Everything runs on your computer. Papers are never uploaded to any cloud service, and analysis results stay in your local results/ folder.

🧠

Real AI Inside

Uses a sentence-transformer embedding model to understand the meaning of text, not just its words — so paraphrased content is caught too.

⚡

Fast & Lightweight

Chunked processing keeps memory low and speed high, even for long papers. Progress is reported live so you always know what's happening.

📊

Transparent Reports

Every score is broken down into semantic vs lexical similarity, matched side-by-side with page numbers — nothing is a black box.

Technology Stack

  • Backend: Python, Flask, PyMuPDF, scikit-learn, sentence-transformers
  • Frontend: Vanilla HTML, CSS and JavaScript — no frameworks, no build step, no tracking
  • Storage: Local filesystem only (uploads/ and results/ folders)
Ethical note: this tool assists academic integrity review. Similarity scores are not proof of misconduct — always apply human judgement.
